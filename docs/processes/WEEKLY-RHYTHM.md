# Weekly Rhythm — Collaboration Cadence

How the three of us stay in sync without burying each other in meetings.

## Standing Cadence

| When | Meeting | Who | Format | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday 9:00 AM CT** | Tech sync | Shain + Brendan | Video | 30 min |
| **Wednesday 10:00 AM CT** | Creative review | Shain + Chip | Video | 45 min |
| **Friday EOD** | Async wins post | Shain → both | Written (Slack/email) | 5 min |
| **1st of month** | Strategy review | All three | Video | 60 min |

## Monday Tech Sync — Agenda

1. What shipped last week (Brendan)
2. What ships this week (Brendan)
3. Blockers / decisions needed (Brendan)
4. Pipeline + lead flow signal (Shain)
5. Action items committed before close

## Wednesday Creative Review — Agenda

1. Drafts in progress walk-through (Chip)
2. Approvals on items in `creative/` since last review (Shain)
3. What lands this week (Chip)
4. Upcoming deadlines (both)
5. Action items committed before close

## Friday Async Wins Post

Shain posts weekly. Format:

```
Week of YYYY-MM-DD
- Wins: [3 bullets]
- Pipeline movement: [#leads, #discovery, #closed]
- Next week's focus: [1-2 sentences]
- Asks: [if any]
```

## Monthly Strategy Review (1st of month)

| Block | Topic | Lead |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:15 | Numbers — pipeline, MRR, $70K progress | Shain |
| 0:15-0:30 | What worked / what didn't | All |
| 0:30-0:45 | Next month priorities | Shain |
| 0:45-0:60 | Risks + decisions | All |

Output: updated `pipeline/PIPELINE-TRACKER.md`, updated `subscription-tracker/SUBSCRIPTION-TRACKER.md`, monthly memo in `financials/monthly-reports/`.

## Communication Channels

| Type | Channel | Response SLA |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent | Phone / SMS to Shain (402-598-5180) | < 1 hr business hours |
| Decisions / approvals | Email | < 24 hrs |
| Day-to-day | Slack DM | same day |

## Norms

- **Pull before you push.** Always.
- **Commit small.** A logo concept = a commit. A tool fix = a commit.
- **Use the commit format.** `[type] description - initials`
- **Friday is for shipping, not starting.** New work starts Monday.
