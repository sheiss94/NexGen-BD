## Status Codes

- **Active** — paying on schedule
- **Trial** — within first 30 days, not yet billed
- **Past Due** — invoice >7 days late
- **Paused** — paying customer on hold (counts as $0 MRR until resume)
- **Churned** — cancelled (record month + reason)
- **Locked-In** — within 6-month non-cancellable initial term

## Term Codes

- **M** — month-to-month (after initial 6-month term ends)
- **6U** — 6-month upfront (4% discount applied)
- **12U** — 12-month upfront (10% discount applied)
- **6M** — initial 6-month commitment, billed monthly
