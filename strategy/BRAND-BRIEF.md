## Tagline

> **Guiding Your Path to Excellence Through Experience.**

## Core Message

> **The gap isn't the software. It's adoption.**

Companies invest in software, automation, and digital tools but struggle to turn those investments into operational ROI. The gap is rarely the tool — it's adoption, standards, process discipline, data quality, and connecting engineering to the shop floor. NexGen bridges that gap.

## Brand Pillars

- **Adoption, not just installation**
- **Standards that scale**
- **Engineering-to-shop ROI**
- **Practical, not theoretical**

## Customer Journey

```
Assess  →  Standardize  →  Activate  →  Scale
```

1. **Assess** — Shop assessment reveals where margin is leaking
2. **Standardize** — Templates, macros, and workflow built for repeatability
3. **Activate** — Membership platform keeps the team using them every month
4. **Scale** — Custom engagements and condition monitoring extend the wins

## Tone & Voice

- **Direct** — no consulting jargon, no fluff
- **Confident** — operators who built this in a real shop, not consultants who studied it
- **Proof-based** — case studies, hard numbers, before/after
- **Shop-floor credible** — speaks panel builder, not MBA
- **Anti-fluff** — explicitly NOT official EPLAN training, NOT unlimited engineering, NOT generic coaching

## Audiences

### Audience 1 — Panel Shop Owners ($5M-$50M revenue)
- Pain: estimating chaos, rework, key-person risk, slow EPLAN adoption
- Buyer: Owner / GM / Executive
- Tier fit: Panel Shop Automation, Premium Combined, Elite

### Audience 2 — OEMs Considering Insourcing
- Pain: vendor dependency, lead times, IP exposure
- Buyer: VP Engineering / VP Operations
- Tier fit: Premium Combined, Elite

### Audience 3 — Engineering Teams & CAD Users (using or adopting EPLAN)
- Pain: project setup, parts data, macros, plot frames, handoff to shop floor
- Buyer: Engineering Manager / Lead Designer
- Tier fit: EPLAN Entry, Premium Combined

### Audience 4 — Manufacturing Leaders & Continuous-Improvement Owners
- Pain: tribal knowledge, lean transformation, scaling from custom to volume
- Buyer: Director of Ops / CI Lead / Production Manager
- Tier fit: Panel Shop Automation, Premium Combined
