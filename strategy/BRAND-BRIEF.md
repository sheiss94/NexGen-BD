# Brand Brief — NexGen Panel Mentoring

**Prepared for:** Chip Thompson Design
**Engagement:** 6 months
**Status:** Working draft — to be reviewed Week 1

## Company

- **Name:** NexGen Panel Mentoring (sister company to Automated Drive Systems)
- **Location:** Omaha, Nebraska
- **Site:** panelmentor.com
- **Parent:** Automated Drive Systems (ADS) — $5-15M industrial control panel manufacturer

## Core Message

> **EPLAN provides the platform. NexGen delivers the payoff.**

We are the operators-turned-mentors who turn EPLAN licenses into shop-floor results.

## Customer Journey

```
Assess  →  Standardize  →  Activate  →  Scale
```

1. **Assess** — Shop Assessment ($5,500) reveals where the money is leaking
2. **Standardize** — EPLAN Sprint ($15K) installs the templates and macros
3. **Activate** — NexGen Core/Pro ($397-$2,500/mo) keeps the team using them
4. **Scale** — Engineering Fix / Build Fix / Condition Monitoring extends the wins

## Tone & Voice

- **Direct** — no consulting jargon, no fluff
- **Confident** — we have done the work, not just studied it
- **Proof-based** — case studies, hard numbers, before/after
- **Shop-floor credible** — we speak panel builder, not MBA

## Audiences

### Audience 1 — Panel Shop Owners ($5M-$50M revenue)
- Pain: estimating chaos, rework, key-person risk, slow EPLAN adoption
- Buyer: Owner / GM
- Hook: "Stop bleeding margin on every panel."

### Audience 2 — OEMs Considering Insourcing
- Pain: vendor dependency, lead times, IP exposure
- Buyer: VP Engineering / VP Operations
- Hook: "Insource panels without the 18-month learning curve."

### Audience 3 — EPLAN Employees & Reps
- Pain: customers buy licenses but never adopt — churn risk
- Buyer: EPLAN regional managers
- Hook: "We make your customers successful so you don't lose them."
