## Two Paths, One Membership Platform

The NexGen Panel Mentoring platform is organized around **two distinct paths**. Members pick one, or combine both with **Premium**. **Elite** is application-only and sits above both paths.

### Path A — EPLAN Adoption & ROI Support
Tactical how-to support for engineers, designers, and CAD/EPLAN users. Practical mentoring to apply the software in a real panel shop.

**Topics:** project setup · parts data structure · macro strategy · plot frames · reports · Pro Panel / 3D · digital manufacturing readiness · engineering-to-production handoff · SmartProduction / downstream manufacturing · drawing standards · reducing wasted setup effort.

> **Not:** unlimited project engineering. Not a replacement for formal EPLAN training. Not affiliated with EPLAN.

### Path B — Panel Shop Automation & Leadership
Strategy and leadership coaching for shop modernization. Software-agnostic — useful on any platform.

**Topics:** Lean panel shop transformation · digital workflow strategy · engineering quality at the source · quote-to-order-to-release discipline · shop-floor standard work · CNC enclosure modification strategy · wire processing automation · training systems · leadership and culture · visual management / Andon · reducing rework · scaling from custom to higher volume.

> **Not:** tactical EPLAN support. This is a leadership forum, not a how-to.
